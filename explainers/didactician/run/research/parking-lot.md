# Parking lot

- Learning styles ("meshing" instruction to visual/verbal learners) lacks adequate evidence; Pashler et al. 2008 found virtually no studies showing the required interaction (https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1539-6053.2009.01038.x). The skill doesn't use the idea, so the page doesn't need it.
- Redundancy effect: adding self-contained duplicate information (e.g. prose restating a self-explanatory figure) can hurt learning (Sweller et al. 2019, Redundancy Effect section).
- Worked example effect and guidance fading: strong evidence, weaker for high-expertise learners (Sweller et al. 2019).
- Meta-analysed contiguity effect sizes (Ginns 2006, Learning and Instruction 16) reported as d ≈ 0.85 in secondary sources; not verified against the original.
- ICAP's evidence base is mainly note taking, concept mapping and self-explaining studies; it is framed as a hypothesis (Chi & Wylie 2014).
- The skill's "about 2–3 interacting elements" working-memory figure (learning-science-patterns.md#L9) is not supported by Sweller et al. 2019, which gives no number; Kirschner, Sweller & Clark 2006 are often quoted as "two to four" (unverified here).
- DESIGN.md itself says the planner/builder split is the weakest justification and must beat a single-agent baseline at roughly 2–3× tokens; untested (DESIGN.md#L12, #L90).
- Privilege boundaries are prompt-level only; namespace writes are detected after the fact, not prevented (DESIGN.md#L448-L458).
- 4C/ID placement (supportive information before a task, procedural information just in time) and desirable difficulties (Bjork & Bjork 2011) are in the skill's references.
