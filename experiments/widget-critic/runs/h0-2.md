# h0-2 · W-02 (w-sizing-sandbox) against the text

Model runs: Node, loading `data.js` and `model.js`, calling `SizingModel.evaluate(SizingData, requests, spare)`. "Defaults" = storefront 4, orders 6, dispatch 2 (payments and notifications 2 each, fixed). `selftest.mjs` passes.

1. **s-09 (k-09 answer) · "try `orders` at 8 and then 12 GB in W-02"**
   What's wrong: the text promises that raising the orders request "can mean another node on the bill", but from the state W-02 opens in, neither 8 nor 12 changes the count. The reader follows the instruction and sees nothing happen. The count rises only if storefront and dispatch are first right-sized to 2.5 and 1.5, and the text never says to do that.
   Evidence: defaults with spare on give orders 6 → 5, 8 → 5, 12 → 5. With spare off: 4, 4, 4. With the other two at use (2, 1), spare on: 4, 4, 4. Only with (2.5, ·, 1.5) and spare off do you get 6 → 3, 8 → 3, 12 → 4 (the selftest's case).

2. **k-04 feedback (option C) · "W-02, below, shows how far it goes."**
   What's wrong: k-04 is about doubling the payments request, but W-02 can't change payments. Only storefront, orders and dispatch have steppers, and the caption itself says payments "stay[s] as the teams set them". Even if the reader could make the change, doubling payments wouldn't add a node, so it wouldn't show the "another node" the feedback describes.
   Evidence: `data.js` ORDER = ['storefront','orders','dispatch']; payments has no `adjustable`. Model: payments 4 (doubled), defaults otherwise → 5 nodes with spare on and 4 with spare off. Both are the same as the defaults.

3. **k-08 feedback (option A) · "at the rush several services would use more than they reserved … (W-02’s warnings show which services)"**
   What's wrong: halve the three requests W-02 lets you change and the warnings name one service, not several. Storefront at 2 and dispatch at 1 are exactly at their use, which doesn't count as over. Payments and notifications can't be halved in W-02, and they wouldn't be over if they were.
   Evidence: (2, 3, 1) with spare on → 4 nodes, over = ["orders"]. With all five halved (2, 3, 1, 1, 1) the model also gives over = ["orders"] only.

4. **s-04, the idle-bar paragraph and the closing answer paragraph vs. the widget's "The rented room", "Reserved {x} of {rented} GB" and the "Nodes" sub-label "what Fennel rents, and pays for in standard mode"**
   What's wrong: W-02 counts each rented node as 13 GB, the room left for Pods. §02 says the machines are 16 GB, and s-04's last paragraph says "about 3 GB of the 16 GB machine is kept back for the system … and Fennel pays for it anyway". So the widget's "rented" room is 15 GB short at defaults, and its idle bar leaves out the third cause the text names. That cause is never visible in W-02. Switching off the spare also shows insurance costing 13 GB, when a node is 16 GB.
   Evidence: `CAPACITY.memory = 13`. Model at defaults with spare on gives rented = 65 GB, which is 5 × 13, but Fennel rents 5 × 16 = 80 GB. Spare off gives rented 52, a difference of 13.

5. **s-04 · "W-02’s idle bar splits the room Fennel rents but doesn’t use into two kinds"**
   What's wrong: the text and the widget describe the bar differently. The text calls it the "idle bar" and says it splits the unused room into two kinds. The widget heads it "The rented room" and draws three segments, "Used at the rush", "Reserved, not used" and "Not reserved", and the word "idle" doesn't appear anywhere in it. A reader looking for an idle bar with two parts has to work out which segment is which.
   Evidence: `SHEET.idle.head = 'The rented room'`; three segments are built in `index.js` (segUsed, segRes, segFree).

6. **s-05 and the second notice in s-04 · "the spare-node switch in W-02" / "Switch off the spare node"**
   What's wrong: the text and the control use different names. The text calls it the spare-node switch, but the control's label is "Keep room to lose a node". Its On/Off state word appears only next to that label. The word "spare" appears on the drawn node and in the "Nodes" sub-line, not on the switch.
   Evidence: `SHEET.spare = 'Keep room to lose a node'`; `STRINGS.spare = 'Spare'` (drawing only).

7. **s-04, first notice · "Set each request to what that service used at the rush."**
   What's wrong: "each request" reads as all five services, but only three can be changed. The reader only finds out from the caption, which comes after the widget. Payments and notifications reserve 2 GB each and used 0.5. (It doesn't change the count: all five at use with spare off → 3, the same as the three at use.)
   Evidence: (2, 5.5, 1) with spare off → 3 nodes, set by processor, reserved 25.5. All five at use → 3 nodes, reserved 19.5. Only three steppers exist.

8. **s-04 notices vs. the prose after W-02 (k-05, the bin-packing paragraph, the idle-bar paragraph)**
   What's wrong: the notices ask "How many nodes does Fennel need now, and what sets that number?" and "What does insurance cost?", but the page never answers them. What W-02 shows is that at use the count drops to 4 (3 + spare) and is set by processor, not memory. That is the chapter's §02 point, that the two resources are counted separately, and nothing after the widget picks it up. The widget's "Set by · Both" (as in the right-sized case) isn't explained anywhere, and neither is the "Set by" readout itself. The third notice ("Push `orders` past 13 GB.") has no question at all.
   Evidence: defaults → 5 nodes, setBy memory (memory alone 4, processor alone 3). At use (2, 5.5, 1) → 4 nodes with spare on, setBy processor (memory alone 2, processor alone 3). Right-sized (2.5, 6, 1.5) → setBy both.

9. **s-04 bin-packing paragraph · "W-02 does it to count the fewest nodes Fennel could manage with"**
   What's wrong: W-02 opens with the spare switch on, so the count it shows by default is the fewest nodes plus one empty node, not the fewest Fennel could manage with.
   Evidence: defaults with spare on → nodes 5, base 4. The "Nodes" readout shows 05.

10. **s-07/s-08 vs. the W-02 data for orders and the first notice**
   What's wrong: W-02 says orders "used 5.5 GB" at the rush, and with the defaults its warnings read "None: every Pod reserves at least what it used". But §07 (Fig. 05) shows orders on Tuesday, after that Friday, going above its 6 GB request up to its 7 GB limit. Following notice 1 sets orders to 5.5 GB, below what the page has just told the reader orders uses, and W-02 gives no warning. §08's point, that requests below use put a service "first in line", is then contradicted by W-02's all-clear at the very setting the page asks for.
   Evidence: `SERVICES.orders.use = 5.5`. (2, 5.5, 1) → over = [], so the warnings read "None: every Pod reserves at least what it used".

11. **k-05 (directly after W-02) · "nodes 05 and 06 are still nearly empty"**
   What's wrong: right under W-02, the text talks about Fennel's nodes 01–06 while W-02 draws "Node 1 … 5". By default W-02's node 4 holds a single notifications Pod and node 5 is the empty spare, which a reader can easily take for Fennel's 05 and 06. Nothing in the lead or the caption says that W-02's nodes are not Fennel's numbered nodes. The caption's "places every Pod afresh" only hints at it.
   Evidence: default placement = [[or1,sf1,di1],[sf2,sf3,di2,di3],[di4,di5,di6,pa1,pa2,no1],[no2],[]]; nodes are labelled String(i + 1).
